LOBSTER HOW-TO

How to eat the whole thing

First time ordering the whole dinner? Here's the order of operations. Take your time - the tide isn't going anywhere.

1

Claws first.

Twist them off and crack them open. The knuckle meat next door is the sweetest bite in the whole lobster.

2

Take the tail.

Twists it away fro the body and snap off the little flippers at the enc.

3

Push, don't dig.

Push the tail meat out in one piece wit your fork. Thumb also works. Nobody's judging.

4

Don't skip the legs.

Bite down gently and squeeze the meat out like a straw. This is where the locals live.
